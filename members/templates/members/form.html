How to prepare your Excel file

  1. Download the Excel template and open the Members tab.
  2. Keep the column headers in row 1 and enter one member per row starting in row 2. Fill in as much information as you can, with at least a last name and first name for each member. You can add missing details or update existing information later.
  3. The app automatically assigns each new member a unique ID, such as MBR-0001, when the record is saved.
  4. Enter birth, wedding, baptism, and spiritual birthday dates as MM/DD/YYYY (for example, 03/11/1991 for March 11, 1991). MM-DD-YYYY also works. Enter ADDRESS, SPOUSE NAME, JOINED TAGC, STATUS, and CIVIL STATUS as you want them to appear on the member record.
  5. Save the completed workbook as an .xlsx file, then select it below and click Import members.
  6. If the import flags a possible duplicate, review the listed row and correct or remove it before trying again.
